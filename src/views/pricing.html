      Questions

      What counts as a request and a file read?

      Every call of the API is a request. Reading a file with the AI model (a roster, an ACARS picture) is also a file read. Both start again at 00:00 UTC every day.

      Can I pay with a card without a PayPal account?

      Yes, where PayPal offers it in your country: choose "Debit or Credit Card" under the PayPal button. PayPal handles the card; this site never sees its number.

      How do I cancel?

      On your account page, at any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then the account moves back to Free. Your API keys keep working.

      Where are my invoices?

      On your account page: one invoice for every payment, to print or save as PDF. PayPal also emails you a receipt.

      Do I need an account to try the API?

      The pages of this site work without one. Your own apps need an API key, from a free account.
