Night Time

Night time of a flight: the aircraft flies the great circle between the two airports at a constant speed from take-off to landing, and the sun's altitude is taken at its position at every moment (SunCalc).

Local time at the aircraft (from its longitude)
Night: before sunrise and from sunset on
Airports: IATA / ICAO / FAA code, or coordinates "lat, lon". An on-block before the off-block is taken as the next day. Without take-off / landing: off-block + 10 min (taxi-out), on-block − 5 min (taxi-in).
