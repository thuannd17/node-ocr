Billing

The paid plans: every PayPal subscription and invoice. A subscription is asked to PayPal again with Sync (PayPal also tells this site by webhook).

Payments are not set up yet

Make a REST app on developer.paypal.com (Sandbox first), then put its keys in .env: PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID, PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET, PAYPAL_ENV=sandbox. For webhooks add one to that app pointing at https://<this site>/api/billing/webhook and put its id in PAYPAL_WEBHOOK_ID. Restart the app; the plans are made in PayPal on the first subscription.
