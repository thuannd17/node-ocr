Roster Calendar

Open one or more .ics files (exported from /upload or from any calendar) to see them by month. Files are read in the browser only, nothing is sent to the server.

or drop .ics files on the page

Duty (from C/I to C/O) is a frame; its flights , sims and hotel sit inside it. A hotel lasts from the event just before it to the event just after it. Striped: a Planned row (STATUS:TENTATIVE). An event past midnight also shows on the next day (in italics). Times without a time zone (local "L" times in the roster) are always shown as written in the file.
