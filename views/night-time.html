Night Time

Night time of a flight: the aircraft flies the great circle between the two airports at a constant speed from off-block to on-block, and the sun's altitude is taken at its position at every moment (SunCalc).

UTC (Z)
UTC (Z)
Airports: IATA / ICAO / FAA code, or coordinates "lat, lon". An on-block before the off-block is taken as the next day.
